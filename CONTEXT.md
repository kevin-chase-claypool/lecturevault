# LectureVault

LectureVault stores source-grounded class reconstructions and turns intentionally scoped sets of those reconstructions into exam reviews.

## Exam scope

**Exam Section**:
A named, course-level assessment scope, such as Exam 1, Exam 2, Exam 3, or Final Exam.
_Avoid_: Exam relevance, unit (unless the syllabus itself names a unit)

**Section Allocation**:
The one or more Exam Sections to which a reconstruction belongs. An unallocated reconstruction is not eligible for a section-scoped review.
_Avoid_: Priority, importance

**Review Scope**:
The Exam Section or selected group of Exam Sections that is permitted to supply reconstructions to one exam review.
_Avoid_: General review filter, exam relevance
